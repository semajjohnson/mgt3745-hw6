## Economic rationale

Part of the same 6 hours recorded in DDR-001, since the analysis and the drafting happened together rather than in sequence. Drafting `COMPARISON.md` and the DDRs myself would have taken longer than reviewing them did, but the reviewing was not free: three times it produced claims drawn from my HW5 builds rather than these ones — that bolt shipped nine unused dependencies, that bolt used no `innerHTML`, that only AI Studio invented off-token colors. Each was wrong for HW6 and each had to be caught by checking the zips. One of those claims was in a draft of `EVALS.md` I had already pasted into the repository before the error surfaced.

The pattern is worth stating plainly: a tool carrying context from a previous week will produce confident, specific, plausible claims about the current week that are false. The cost of delegation here was not the drafting, it was the auditing, and the auditing had to happen against primary sources rather than against the draft.
