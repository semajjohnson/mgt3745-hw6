## Profile 1: Rishika, Specifier
- **Context.** Full-time working professional who travels casually [known]. Shares trip moments by texting friends directly, many times a day while traveling, instead of posting to social media [known]. Sends photos, short stories, and places he loved, in the moment [known]. Likes knowing what his friends are up to too [known]. Has little patience for long reviews or blogging [assumed]. Source: Rishika, INT-01.
- **Job statement.** When something funny or worth remembering happens on a trip, I want to share it with my friends right away without the pressure of a public post, so I can bring them along on what I am doing.
- **Success looks like.** His friends on the trip and at home see the moment the same day, and it stays together with the rest of the trip instead of scattered across text threads [assumed]. His friends can respond without feeling pressured to reply to every message [assumed].

## Profile 2: Rishika, Specifier
- **Context.** Active Instagram and TikTok user who posts trip highlights to her story often [known]. Thinks about what to photograph for posting while she travels [known]. Looks up popular activities and meals on social media when planning [known]. Cares about how her feed looks and takes deliberate, polished photos [known]. Wants to share her trip in more depth than a short caption allows [assumed]. Source: Rishika, INT-02.
- **Job statement.** When I am exploring a new place, I want to find the spots worth going to and document them, so I can share a trip I am proud of.
- **Success looks like.** She can show a finished trip with more said about each place than a caption holds [assumed]. People who see it can use it to plan their own trips [assumed].

## Profile 3: Semaj, Reviewer
- **Context.** Picks up new things socially rather than from algorithms: through friends, shared sessions, and social posts, without asking for recommendations directly [known, from music]. Trusts recommendations from people whose taste he already knows [known, from music]. Uses community threads to find niche things when friends don't have the answer [known, from music]. His frustration is finding and organizing what he has already found, not the quality of suggestions [known, from music]. That he finds trip ideas the same way he finds music is **[assumed]**: every travel claim in this profile is a transfer from music research and has not been tested. Source: Semaj, Interview 1 and Interview 2.
- **Job statement.** When I want to find somewhere new to go, I want it to come from a real person whose taste I already trust, so I can go without second-guessing whether it is for me.
- **Success looks like.** He finds a trip from someone he follows, saves the places from it, and can find them again when he plans his own trip [assumed].
