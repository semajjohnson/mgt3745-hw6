## Original Problem Statement (<owner>, HW1)
Traveling is one of people's most beloved hobbies, and is a time where they are constantly taking pictures to share with the world. However, in this day and age, social media is more judgemental of the content you post. Certain platforms are too formal, while others are too serious. People take trips and love sharing about them with friends on social and text, but never get to simply put thoughts out there in a quick way and often forget details since they were never recorded.

## Reframed for the Team
**Reframe 1: who owns the problem.**
The original belongs to one traveler sharing outward to an audience. For a group, the problem belongs to the whole group. The details of a shared trip are scattered across several people's camera rolls and group chats, with nobody holding the full record. The traveler is no longer a creator worried about an audience but someone contributing to a shared trip.

**Reframe 2: what counts as solved.**
The original counts it solved when a traveler can post quickly without judgment. Reframed, it is solved when a detail can still be found later. Anyone in the group can find where a photo was taken and what was happening, without that moment ever having been posted publicly. Posting is optional; recall is the measure.

**What we kept.** Quick capture in the moment and sharing with friends. A group can choose to share a trip to the main feed, so the social side of the original problem is still there.

**What we dropped.** The single creator posting to an audience as the center of the problem. Sharing is now something a group chooses to do with a finished trip, not the reason the app exists. That also answers the judgment problem from the original: the group decides together what the outside world sees, so no one posts alone into a judgmental feed.

## Outcome
Members of a trip group can find where a photo was taken, what was happening, and who added it after the trip, from a record they built during it.

## Scope
- **In:** creating a trip and inviting members by link, any member adding a photo with a note and place, a main feed of the viewer's trips, finding a moment by place, date, member, or note, and sharing a finished trip when the group chooses.
- **Out:** editing photos, direct messaging, and connecting to other social platforms.

## Constraints
- No photos or personal details of anyone outside the team enter any tool or repository during the course
- Course stack: Cloudflare Workers, D1, static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Build time in Phase 2
  
## Stakeholders
- **Primary:** members of a small group trip: the person who organizes it and the friends who add to it.
- **Secondary:** followers/friends/family that see the trip on their main feed, owners of the businesses and places tagged in shared trips
